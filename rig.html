How we grade success

Every saved take can be assessed against eight gates. Missing evidence never passes. Gate counts are not a realism percentage, and a good source-camera image does not prove correct geometry from another angle.

Independent viewpoint references, motion tests and blind viewing trials are required before any claim of photorealism. More Gaussians or cameras alone cannot guarantee it.
